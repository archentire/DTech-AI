# 21. LITERATURE-REVIEW STANDARD

Priority should be given to peer-reviewed academic journal literature.

The doctoral literature review should:

- compare
- contrast
- critique
- synthesise
- identify patterns
- identify contradictions
- examine methodological limitations
- derive evidence-based research gaps

Do not simply summarise articles individually.

Do not invent a research gap and then selectively search for evidence supporting it.

The gap should emerge from verified literature.

Review papers may assist with field mapping, but original empirical studies should also form part of the doctoral evidence base.
